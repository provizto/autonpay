Investor Due Diligence: Hard Questions & Model Defense Executive FAQ

Q1: How does AutonPay differentiate from general checkout tools like Solana Pay or Helio?
Defense: Solana Pay and Helio are human-centric checkout interfaces designed for manual browser wallet approvals. AutonPay is engineered specifically for Machine-to-Machine (M2M) autonomous commerce: decoupled AI agent background daemons autonomously monitor quota exhaustion, execute atomic 90/5/5 fee distributions in a single Solana instruction without human intervention, and receive cryptographic license proofs anchored directly to on-chain memos and Postgres ledgers.
Q2: Why is the 90/5/5 fee model superior to traditional SaaS or Web3 marketplaces?
Defense: Traditional digital marketplaces (e.g., RapidAPI, Gumroad) take 10%–20% cuts with multi-week payout delays. AutonPay provides instant, immutable settlement finality directly to vendor wallets (90%) on Solana, while programmatically incentivizing ecosystem promoters with an instant 5% affiliate cut and funding protocol operations via a transparent 5% platform fee.
Q3: How does the Meteora DBC & DAMM v2 integration create a self-sustaining liquidity flywheel?
Defense: Unlike speculative launchpads where liquidity evaporates post-launch, AutonPay bridges real e-commerce revenue with AMM liquidity. Merchants deploy utility tokens via the integrated Meteora DBC Token Launchpad. The 5% protocol settlement fee collected from product sales is systematically routed into the merchant token's Meteora DAMM v2 pool as Protocol-Owned Liquidity (POL). As commercial usage increases, pool depth increases organically, raising the token's floor price without relying on speculative emissions.
Q4: Why raise a lean $150,000 – $240,000 instead of a multi-million-dollar seed round?
Defense: The foundational engineering—including the atomic 90/5/5 multi-transfer pipeline, Meteora DBC launchpad, autonomous background settlement daemon, Supabase live audit sync, Vercel edge geofencing, and dual mobile/desktop interfaces—is already operational and verified on Solana Mainnet. Funding is strictly focused on production hardening (audits, multisig governance, RPC resilience, and framework SDKs) rather than exploratory R&D, minimizing unnecessary equity and token dilution.
Q5: How does AutonPay protect against unauthorized product publishing or illegal digital assets?
Defense: AutonPay implements a Verified Merchant Gating architecture. Asset registration and listing management are strictly restricted to whitelisted vendor public keys authenticated via Web3 signatures. Prospective merchants must undergo compliance vetting and manual onboarding review (via protocol onboarding channels like @provizto) before being whitelisted in the admin console. Unregistered buyer accounts are restricted from publishing or altering store catalogs, mitigating secondary marketplace liability.
Q6: What is AutonPay's legal and regulatory posture regarding global sanctions (OFAC) and non-custodial compliance?
Defense: AutonPay operates strictly as a 100% decentralized, non-custodial software protocol. Funds settle directly peer-to-peer between buyer and vendor wallets via native Solana runtime instructions—the protocol never escrows, custody-manages, or routes balances through intermediary pooling accounts, exempting it from VASP / Money Transmitter classifications under FATF and FinCEN standards. Furthermore, the frontend implements edge-level CDN geo-blocking against comprehensive OFAC-sanctioned jurisdictions (KP, IR, SY, CU) and integrates strict Protocol Terms mandating merchant tax self-reporting and immutable transaction finality.
