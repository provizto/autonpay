Investor Due Diligence: Hard Questions & Model Defense Executive FAQ

Q1: How does AutonPay differentiate from general checkout tools like Solana Pay or Helio?
Defense: Solana Pay and Helio are human-centric payment modals designed for web browsers and manual wallet approvals. AutonPay is engineered specifically for Machine-to-Machine (M2M) autonomous commerce: AI agent daemons autonomously track quota depletion, sign transactions via a dedicated Gas Tank, split fees atomically 90/5/5 in a single instruction, and receive cryptographically verified license keys and API endpoints directly into their runtime environment without human intervention.
Q2: Why is the 90/5/5 fee model superior to traditional SaaS or Web3 marketplaces?
Defense: Traditional digital marketplaces (e.g. RapidAPI, Gumroad) take 10%–20% cuts with multi-week settlement delays. AutonPay provides instant settlement finality directly to vendor wallets (90%) on Solana Devnet and Mainnet, while programmatically incentivizing ecosystem promoters with an instant 5% affiliate cut and funding protocol operations via a transparent 5% platform fee.
Q3: Why raise a lean $150,000 – $240,000 instead of a multi-million-dollar seed round?
Defense: The foundational engineering—including the atomic 90/5/5 multi-transfer pipeline, Gas Tank tracking, Supabase audit synchronization, and dual mobile/desktop dashboards—is already operational on Solana Devnet. Funding is strictly focused on production hardening (audits, multisig governance, RPC resilience, and framework SDKs) rather than exploratory R&D, minimizing unnecessary equity and token dilution.
Q4: How does AutonPay protect against unauthorized product publishing or illegal digital assets?
Defense: AutonPay implements a Verified Merchant Gating architecture. Asset registration and listing management are strictly restricted to whitelisted vendor public keys authenticated via Web3 signatures. Unregistered buyer wallets can purchase licenses and execute agent commands, but are restricted from publishing or altering store catalogs.
Q5: How does the protocol generate organic cashflow to sustain operations post-funding?
Defense: Every on-chain settlement triggers an atomic 5% protocol fee routed directly to the Protocol Treasury. As autonomous AI agents scale micro-transactions across LLM inferences, vector database queries, and data feeds, AutonPay captures predictable, high-velocity cash flow in SOL/USDC to fund RPC infrastructure and ongoing protocol development without requiring continual equity financing.
