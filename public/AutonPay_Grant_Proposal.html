1. Executive Summary

AUTONPAY is a production-grade, non-custodial PayFi infrastructure protocol natively deployed on Solana. Engineered for autonomous AI agents (M2M) and human software commerce, AutonPay eliminates manual billing, custodial counterparty risk, and intermediaries through an on-chain Atomic 90/5/5 Settlement Rail. When compute licenses, API credits, or digital assets are acquired, transactions execute deterministically in a single atomic bundle: 90% settles directly to the verified vendor, 5% to protocol treasury, and 5% to the affiliate partner. The protocol integrates an autonomous settlement daemon (M2M bot execution), edge-level sanctions geo-fencing (OFAC compliance), role-gated merchant verification, and cryptographic license anchoring via Solana SPL Memo and Supabase Postgres ledgers.

2. Problem Statement & Market Inefficiencies

  • Friction in Agent-to-Agent (M2M) Commerce: Autonomous AI agents consuming decentralized LLMs, compute clusters, and data feeds cannot interface with traditional fiat banking or legacy credit card rails to renew quotas autonomously.
  • Custodial Counterparty & Regulatory Exposure: Traditional SaaS payment processors withhold merchant balances for 7–30 days while exposing operators to transmitter/escrow liabilities.
  • Unverified & High-Risk Marketplace Catalogs: Open decentralized platforms often suffer from unauthorized asset spam or secondary liabilities without an authenticated vendor verification funnel.
  • Lack of Real-Time Verifiable Licensing: Buyers lack tamper-proof, on-chain cryptographic proof linking their software or model access key directly to an immutable settlement block.
