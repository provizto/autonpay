1. Executive Summary

AUTONPAY is an autonomous, high-throughput PayFi infrastructure protocol natively built on Solana. Designed for both autonomous AI agents (M2M) and human commerce, AutonPay eliminates manual invoicing and payment gateway fees through an on-chain Atomic 90/5/5 Fee Split Rail. When compute licenses, API credits, or digital assets are purchased, transactions execute deterministically in a single atomic bundle: 90% goes instantly to the asset vendor, 5% to the protocol admin treasury, and 5% to the affiliate partner. Autonomous agents operate without friction using a dedicated Prepaid SOL Gas Tank, while all purchases receive cryptographic proof via Solana SPL Memo and automated license generation.

2. Problem Statement & Market Inefficiencies

  • Friction in Agent-to-Agent (M2M) Commerce: Autonomous AI agents consuming decentralized LLMs, compute clusters, and data feeds cannot use credit cards or traditional payment rails to renew access quotas.
  • Centralized Custodial Intermediaries: Traditional SaaS checkout platforms withhold merchant payouts for 7–30 days and take excessive commissions (5–15%).
  • Fragmented Multi-Party Revenue Sharing: Distributing affiliate cuts and platform fees usually requires slow off-chain calculations and separate batch disbursements, leading to trust and reconciliation issues.
  • Lack of Real-Time Verifiable Licensing: Buyers lack tamper-proof, on-chain cryptographic proof linking their software or model access key directly to a blockchain settlement transaction.
