1. Executive Summary

ZONIQFI is a modular DeFi & PayFi infrastructure protocol on Solana bridging retail commerce with high-velocity on-chain settlement. Utilizing Solana Transaction v1 architecture (4,096-byte atomic payload) and Pyth Network Oracles for real-time valuation, the protocol combines MEV-protected liquidity swaps via Jito Block Engine, automated yield vaults, and a non-custodial PayFi Gateway. Merchants receive 100% fiat payouts directly via personal QRIS, while an automated on-chain Prepaid SOL Gas Tank debits and splits protocol fees across four on-chain vaults with hard guardrail protections.

2. Problem Statement & Market Inefficiencies

  • Onboarding & Gas Friction for Merchants: Web2 merchants in emerging markets cannot navigate complex token swaps, crypto volatility, or gas management simply to accept payments.
  • Pervasive MEV Exploitation: Arbitrage and sandwich bots in public mempools extract substantial value from retail traders.
  • Custodial Intermediary Risk: Centralized payment gateways charge heavy fees (2–4%) and impose multi-day settlement delays on merchant revenue.
  • Inflationary Farm Vulnerabilities: Traditional AMMs rely on hyper-inflationary token emissions rather than real-world commercial fee cashflow.
